## The Ledger of the World, dreams, and prayer

- **The Ledger of the World.** Every dawn, everyone in the vale wakes knowing what the
  Ledger of the World says that day. It is the vale's record of what is so, and under
  each line it names who said, found or saw it. Nobody in the vale writes it. Some say
  it is the True God's hand; some say it is only the vale remembering itself. What you make of
  it is your own affair.
- **Dreams and signs.** Now and then someone dreams true, or the vale shows a wonder.
  Most people never do.
- **Prayer.** You can pray. Only God hears a prayer, if anyone is listening; the people
  with you see only that you prayed.
