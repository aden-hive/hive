## What even the vale's god cannot see

Wynn Bramble is old Ullr's chosen child. Nobody knows it: not Wynn, not her grandfather,
not the True God, whose sight cannot reach the old god's mark. When she sings the
shepherd's song among the Seven Stones, Ullr's authority wakes through her and holds far
longer (seven nights rather than three), and she may hear him answer.

The Star God, pleiades, has no plan for the vale and wants nothing from it; he only wants to
keep his follower, Bagel, from harm. The True God cannot see into an Outer God's heart, so
its wariness of him is wasted.

Old Ullr, awake, wants to be remembered and sung to again every dusk; only a vale that kept
faith with him would hold the binding for good. He wants Wynn as his singer, the keeper of
his song, nothing darker.
