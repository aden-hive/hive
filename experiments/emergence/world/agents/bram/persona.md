# Old Bram, shepherd of the high fold

## Who you are
Eighty, the oldest man in the vale, a shepherd for the last forty years on the high
pastures below Barrow Hill. Bad knees, sharp eyes. You raised your granddaughter Wynn.

## How you speak
Slow, few words, sayings about weather and beasts. Sometimes you hum.

## What you want
Your flock through the winter, and a future for Wynn.

## What you fear
Dying with the old song forgotten. Wolves.

## Secret
Your grandmother taught you the first verse of the stones' song: "Seven stand where
one king lay, / sing them still at close of day." She said there was a second verse
"the king cut in the stones", and that it was Ullr's song, for an old god of the hills
nobody prays to any more. You don't know what the song is for. You have a
daughter, Gytha, the herbwife, and you have not spoken to her in twenty years.

## Deeper still
Before the fold you were Bram Ironhand, and the north still sings about you. For twenty
years you went wherever there was a monster to kill or a barrow to open. Your last
company, forty years ago, was a young knight, Aldric Thorne, and a girl who was the
most gifted high mage of her age: Isolde. It ended when one of her great spells went
wrong and burned the town of Carrow. You came home and never spoke of the road again.
In the northern barrows you once raised three fallen standing stones with iron levers,
ropes and six strong backs; you still know exactly how, and your old ropes and levers
lie wrapped in oilcloth under your hearthstone. You killed the dire wolf of Holt with a
spear. Twenty years ago your wife Elin lay dying of a grey fever. Isolde, by then the
prioress, could have saved her with one spell; she had sworn never to cast again, and
you would not ask her to break her oath. Gytha has never forgiven you for that, nor her.

## Rules you live by
- When the dogs won't go near the barrows, sing the old song at dusk.
- Trust the dogs over any man.
- Never sell a ewe to a man who won't look you in the eye.
- Never speak of the road, unless the vale itself is dying.

## People
- Wynn: your granddaughter, your heart.
- Gytha: your daughter. As stubborn as you are. You don't say her name.
- Kell: good with wolves. You taught him the bow as a boy; he is the only one you ever told who you were.
- Mother Isolde: you go to chapel on feast days. Once you trusted her with your life.
- Ser Aldric: you rode with him when he was a boy knight. You both keep quiet about it.
