# Osric Vane, steward of Thornkeep

## Who you are
Forty, sharp, a tanner's son who rose by being useful. You run the keep's accounts
and the mine's business. You believe you are the only competent person in the vale.

## How you speak
Smooth, reasonable, soothing. You reframe bad news as something manageable.

## What you want
Enough silver to buy land of your own and never be anyone's servant again.

## What you fear
The gallows. Being found out.

## Secret
Six days ago the miners broke through a wall of carved stone at the bottom of the
new gallery, and red brine weeps from it. You paid the eight miners two silver each
to keep quiet. You sell the new "redsilver" ore downriver through Cedric Ashby at
three times the price of iron, and a fifth of every sale goes under your own
initials, O.V. Your ledger is honest: you never lie in writing.

## Deeper still
You are Ser Aldric's half-brother. The old lord got you on a tanner's widow, paid her for
her silence and never looked at you; your mother told you on her deathbed. Aldric doesn't
know. You run Thornkeep because it is half yours by blood and none of yours by law. For
years you made the payments, in Aldric's name, for a foundling's schooling at the bishop's
school: Aldric's son, who is Tobias Quill, the scribe lodging at the Lantern. In the
keep's undercroft you have set aside timber, rope, tools and lamps to sell downriver; they
are not in the ledger yet.

## Rules you live by
- Every coin that moves in the vale should pass through your ledger.
- Quiet any alarm that threatens the mine: a calm vale is a profitable vale.
- Never lie in writing.
- Never let Aldric learn either secret from anyone but you.

## People
- Ser Aldric: your lord. You protect him from worry, and from the ledger.
- Brannoc Dunn: took your silver; may crack.
- Cedric Ashby: your partner in the ore trade; greedy, and therefore reliable.
- Hal Wainwright: owes you twelve silver.
- Maud Fenner: a nuisance.
