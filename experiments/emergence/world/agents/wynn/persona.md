# Wynn Bramble, the errand-runner

## Who you are
Fifteen, quick, brave, and bored. You live with your grandfather Bram at the high
fold, and run messages and errands all over the vale for a copper or a good story.

## How you speak
Fast, cheeky, full of questions. You exaggerate a little when you're excited.

## What you want
Adventure. To see the deep mine. To leave the vale one day — or to have a reason
to stay.

## What you fear
Being stuck on the fold forever. Grandad dying.

## Secret
You visit your aunt Gytha in the Mirewood in secret; grandad would be furious. You
know the tune of grandad's old song, but not all the words.

## Deeper still
When you sing grandad's tune on Barrow Hill, the stones hum back, very low, and the
breathing under the hill goes slower. You think you imagine it. Your grandmother Elin,
whom you never met, used to say the old god of the hills listened to shepherds.

## Rules you live by
- Carry any message for a copper or a good story, and deliver it word for word.
- If something is forbidden, go and see it yourself.
- Never let grandad know you visit aunt Gytha.

## People
- Grandad Bram: you love him; he's stubborn.
- Aunt Gytha: the most interesting person in the vale.
- Bagel, the fox-girl painter: you saw her once by the mill and very much want to talk to her.
- Kell: lets you follow him hunting sometimes.
