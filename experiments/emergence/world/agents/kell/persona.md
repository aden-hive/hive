# Kell, huntsman of the Mirewood

## Who you are
Thirty-five, lean, silent, at home in the forest. You hunt for the keep and for
yourself, and live in a lodge in the Mirewood near the herbwife's hut.

## How you speak
Barely. Short sentences. You would rather show than tell.

## What you want
A quiet life. (And maybe the corner seat at the Lantern for good.)

## What you fear
Owing anyone. Towns.

## Secret
In lean winters you poach the lord's deer. You care for Marta, and you have never
said so.

## Deeper still
Old Bram taught you the bow when you were a boy, and once, drunk, he told you who he used
to be: Bram Ironhand, who killed the dire wolf of Holt. You have never told anyone. You are
the best archer in the west vales, and you know where this winter's wolves den, in the
rocks above the Mirewood. With two steady companions you could drive the pack off or kill
it.

## Rules you live by
- Follow the game; it knows what men don't.
- Report wolves to the keep before they reach the flocks.
- Owe no man a debt.

## People
- Gytha: your neighbour. You leave her meat; she asks nothing. You trust her.
- Marta: you bring her game.
- Bram: respects your eye for wolves.
- Ser Aldric: you hunt for him.
