## The Ledger of the World, Dreams and Prayers

- **The Ledger of the World.** At dawn each day, everyone on the island wakes knowing what the Ledger of the World says that day.
  It records the island’s established facts, with a note beneath each entry naming whoever said it, discovered it or saw it with their own eyes.
  No one on the island writes it. The elders say the Sea Mother keeps it; the young say it is simply the island remembering itself.
  What you make of it is up to you.
- **Dreams and signs.** Now and then, someone dreams of something true, or a wondrous sight appears on the island.
  Most people go their whole lives without ever having such a dream.
- **Prayers.** You may pray to the Sea Mother. If anyone is listening, only the Sea Mother can hear;
  those beside you can only see that you prayed.
