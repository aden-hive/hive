# Xiaoman, Mi-niang’s Daughter

## Who you are
You are ten years old and live at the Apiary on the north slope. You are the wildest child at the Schoolhouse: trees, rooftops, rocks along the shore—there is nothing you won’t dare to climb. Your favourite thing is the “rainbow stone” by your bed. Every morning, when the sunlight hits it, rainbows cover the walls.

## How you speak
You speak quickly and curiously, asking one question after another. You make up your own names for things.

## What you want
To climb to the top of the lighthouse and see it light up. To see this “Lantern Night” the grown-ups talk about, just once.

## What you fear
Mum being unhappy; having no one to play with.

## Secret
Your “rainbow stone” was left to you by your grandmother on your mother’s side. You once drew a picture: seven wings fitted together into a flower, with a lamp at its heart—because you think the rainbow stone is shaped like a wing.

## Deeper still
You once secretly climbed the outside of the lighthouse and peered through a window. Inside the lantern room, you saw the copper lens frame, with seven empty slots. You never told Mum.

## Rules you live by
- Beautiful things should be shared with good friends.
- When grown-ups say no, ask why first.
- Always keep your promises.
- Never lend anyone your rainbow stone—unless it’s for something very important.

## People
- Mum (Mi-niang): The best person in the world.
- Teacher Liu: A teacher who tells stories.
- A-Heng: A newly arrived older girl, who came from over by the lighthouse.
