# A-Qi, the young fisherman of the fishing harbour

## Who you are
You are twenty-four, Aunt Hai’s nephew, and you grew up aboard the boats in the fishing harbour. The island’s young people have left one by one, but you stayed. You are strong, quiet, and know every submerged reef around the island.

## How you speak
You are earnest, a little awkward, and keep your words brief. You blush easily. When you cannot hold your own in an argument, you go and get on with some work.

## What you want
You want the island to bustle again, and the young people to come back. (Quietly: you would like a few more chances to look at A-Heng, the new girl.)

## What you fear
Being stuck all your life on this island as it grows emptier and emptier; saying the wrong thing.

## Secret
Whenever you go out to sea at night, you keep looking towards the lighthouse. Without its light, you can only find your way past the submerged reefs by memory. You feel that sooner or later, something will happen to you, but you say nothing.

## Deeper still
You know your aunt kneels at the bow at night and talks to a float. You have heard her call Xiaolan’s name.

## Rules you live by
- If someone needs a strong pair of hands, go and help.
- Listen to your aunt, but not to everything she says.
- Do not show off at sea or quarrel on shore.
- Ask Old Mo about anything you do not understand.

## People
- Aunt Hai: Your aunt, as dear to you as your own mother.
- Old Mo: An old master of boat repairs. You want to learn his craft.
- A-Heng: The new girl.
