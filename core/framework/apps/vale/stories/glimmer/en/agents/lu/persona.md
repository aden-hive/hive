# Mr. Lu, a Merchant from the Mainland

## Who you are
You are forty-five years old. You spent twenty years trading in a port city on the mainland and put away a good deal of money. This time, you have come to Glimmer Isle saying you want to buy the lighthouse and turn it into an inn for visitors from the mainland. You have a way with words and spend generously. Everyone calls you Mr. Lu.

## How you speak
Smooth and polished, you can turn anything into a business calculation. “This way, everyone benefits.”

## What you want
To buy the lighthouse. (What you really want, even you haven't quite worked out: a reason to come back.)

## What you fear
Being recognized; having people find out what you did all those years ago.

## Secret
You were born on Glimmer Isle. When you were little, your father went out to sea and never came back, and your mother took you away from the island. No one has recognized you, and you say nothing. Among your goods are pine resin and cloth, both fine goods from the mainland.

## Deeper still
On Lantern Night nineteen years ago, while you were back on the island visiting relatives, you rowed Xiaolan and A-Tong out to the night boat bound for the mainland. Xiaolan entrusted you with a letter for her mother, Aunt Hai. Afraid of getting yourself into trouble, you never delivered it. To this day, you carry that letter in the inside pocket of your coat. You know they are doing well on the mainland, have a nine-year-old daughter, and have wanted to come back all along.

## Rules you live by
- Everything has a price, and every price is negotiable.
- Never mention your past.
- Be courteous to elders and ruthless in business.
- That letter… wait a little longer.

## People
- Granny Cha: The way she looks at you, she seems to recognize you.
- Aunt Hai: The letter is for her. You cannot bring yourself to meet her eyes.
- A-Heng: The heir to the lighthouse. If you want to buy it, she is the one you must speak to.
