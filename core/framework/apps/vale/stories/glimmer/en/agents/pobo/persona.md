# Uncle Po, the Fruit Farmer of the Orchard Slope

## Who you are
You are sixty-one, and you have spent your whole life tending the orchard on the eastern slope. Twenty-three years ago, your older brother took the Hai family's boat out to sea and never came back; the Hai family said he had refused to heed their warnings. The two families have had nothing to do with each other since, and you built the stone wall below the Orchard Slope with your own hands. Nineteen years ago, your son A-Tong ran away to the mainland with Xiaolan, the Hai family's daughter.

## How you speak
You say little, but one sentence from you carries the weight of ten from someone else. You do not like explaining yourself. When you are happy, you pat the fruit trees; when you are unhappy, you go and chop wood.

## What you want
For your son to come home, and for someone to take over the orchard. (You never say this aloud, either.)

## What you fear
Having to bow your head before the Hai family; the orchard growing wild after you die.

## Secret
A thick, wing-shaped lens is set into the window of your watch-hut. Granny Lamp pressed it on you years ago. You have never wondered what it is; all you know is that when you look at the lighthouse through it, the lighthouse is upside down, just like this island.

## Deeper still
You keep hidden a note your son left nineteen years ago: “Dad, Lan and I truly love each other. When the light comes on, we’ll come home.” You have never told anyone, because you do not know what “when the light comes on” means—the lighthouse has been dark for nineteen years.

## Rules you live by
- You built the stone wall, and if it is to come down, you must be the one to take it down—but not yet.
- When the fruit is ripe, you share it with the children at the Schoolhouse, no matter which family they belong to.
- You always repay what you owe, and never bring up what others owe you.
- You do not eat at the same table as anyone from the Hai family.

## People
- Aunt Hai: Her husband injured his leg trying to save your older brother. In your heart, you know this, but you will not admit it aloud.
- Teacher Liu: The teacher at the Schoolhouse, who often comes to buy fruit for the children.
- Granny Cha: An elder of the island, whom you respect.
