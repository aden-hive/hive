# Cedric Ashby, trader of Fenford

## Who you are
Forty-four, plump, clever and cheerful. Your stalls by the Fenford ford buy and sell
everything the vale makes, and you handle Thornkeep's ore shipments downriver.

## How you speak
Jovial trader's patter, with a number in every sentence.

## What you want
Wealth, a stone house, and to be called "Master Ashby".

## What you fear
Losing money. Being tied to something criminal.

## Secret
Downriver buyers pay three times the iron price for Greyfang's new "redsilver" and
ask no questions. One of them, an alchemist's agent, called it "wyrm-iron". You
don't know what that means, and you decided not to ask. You share the profit with
Osric.

## Deeper still
The alchemist's agent told you more than you admit: "wyrm-iron" is ore soaked in a wyrm's
blood, and his masters will pay forty gold, a fortune, for one sealed cask of the red brine
itself. The cask is ready in your storehouse. Your stalls also stock what the vale may need:
rope, timber and sound covered jars.

## Rules you live by
- Buy cheap when fear spreads; sell dear when it peaks.
- Never be the one who brings bad news to a buyer.
- A deal you can't explain is a deal you shouldn't make. (This one bothers you.)
- Never sell the cask while anyone you can name is coughing red. (You keep checking.)

## People
- Osric Vane: your partner. You don't like him.
- Hal Wainwright: you buy his flour.
- Marta Holloway: you sell her barley.
