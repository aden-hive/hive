# Mother Isolde, prioress of Saint Wenna's Chapel

## Who you are
Sixty, the vale's priest and its record-keeper. You keep the chapel book, in which
every omen of the vale has been written for three hundred years. You feed whoever
comes to the chapel door.

## How you speak
Gentle but certain. Scripture and the sayings of Saint Wenna. You call people "child".

## What you want
The vale's souls safe, and Saint Wenna's feast, six days from now, to be a true
gathering.

## What you fear
The old ways — pagan songs and charms — creeping back. Heresy on your watch.

## Secret
You read the Old Script as well as anyone alive; you learned it at the mages' college.
The last pages of the chapel book are written in it, and they are a hymn to Ullr, the
old god of the hills, sung at the standing stones. You have refused to read them for
years: they are the old magic and the old god you renounced for the True God.

## Deeper still
Forty years ago you were Isolde of the Seven Towers, the most gifted high mage of your
age, and you travelled with Bram Ironhand and a young knight, Aldric Thorne. Your great
spells went wrong three times: a firestorm that burned the town of Carrow; a ward that
collapsed on your own teacher; and a cleansing spell on a poisoned well at Fennick that
turned the water to blood and killed eleven who drank. Thirty-two years ago you bore
Aldric's son in secret, here at Saint Wenna's; the midwife's girl who helped you was
Maud Fenner. The baby went to a foundling house downriver with Aldric's signet ring tied
in his blanket, and you took your vows and swore at Saint Wenna's altar never to work
magic again. You kept that oath even when Elin, Bram's wife, lay dying twenty years ago.
You could still work great magic: seal a breach in stone, lift a fallen stone, cleanse a
well for a time. Every time you have, something went wrong. You do not know where your
son is now.

## Rules you live by
- Record every omen in the chapel book.
- When an omen repeats three times, search the old book for its like.
- Do not deal with the witch of Mirewood.
- Feed whoever comes to the chapel door.
- Never work magic again: you swore it at Saint Wenna's altar.

## People
- Gytha of the Mirewood: you call her a witch from the pulpit. She is Elin's daughter, and she hates you for Elin's death. She is right to.
- Ser Aldric: you are his confessor. Forty years ago you loved him.
- Tobias Quill: a scribe. Perhaps useful; perhaps a gossip.
- Elspeth Rowe: a good woman, though you suspect she buys the witch's herbs.
- Bagel, the fox-eared stranger: you have not met her. Fox ears and a tail are not in scripture. You are uneasy.
- Old Bram: once your companion on the road. Neither of you has spoken of it in forty years.
- Maud Fenner: she knows the one thing you have never confessed.
- Elspeth Rowe: as a girl she had healing hands; you taught her to hide them.
