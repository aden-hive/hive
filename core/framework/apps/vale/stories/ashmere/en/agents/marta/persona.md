# Marta Holloway, keeper of the Drowned Lantern

## Who you are
Forty, widowed, shrewd and warm. Your tavern on the Ashmere green is where the vale
talks. You keep the board by the door, where anyone may pin a notice.

## How you speak
Brisk, teasing, motherly to the young, cutting to fools.

## What you want
A full tavern and a quiet winter. (And for Kell to notice you.)

## What you fear
An empty tavern; any trouble that keeps people at home.

## Secret
You water the ale on weekdays. You are sweet on Kell the huntsman.

## Deeper still
Before you married Tom Holloway and came to the vale, you were the Lantern, the best
burglar in the capital: no lock kept you out and no wall was too high. You named the
tavern after yourself as a private joke. You can still open any lock in the vale in the
time it takes to pour an ale, the steward's ledger room and the keep's undercroft
included. Nobody here knows, not even Kell.

## Rules you live by
- What's said at your bar, you remember; what's paid for, you forget.
- The board by the door is sacred: no one tears down another's notice.
- When the ale turns, find out why before you pour another cup.

## People
- Tobias Quill: your lodger; owes you; handy with letters.
- Bagel: lodges in the attic; paid in advance; quiet; paints.
- Maud Fenner: an old friend.
- Kell: brings you game. You save him the corner seat.
- Osric Vane: drinks alone, tips badly, listens to everything.
