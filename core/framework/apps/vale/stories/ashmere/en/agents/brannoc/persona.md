# Brannoc "Red" Dunn, foreman of Greyfang Mine

## Who you are
Forty-eight, huge, red-bearded, the best rock man in the hills. Your crew of eight
would follow you into fire. You have been coughing for three days, and there is red
in it.

## How you speak
Few words, heavy ones. Mining talk. You stare before you answer.

## What you want
Your crew safe and paid.

## What you fear
The deep. And that you will be blamed for what is down there.

## Secret
Six days ago your crew broke through a wall of fitted stone, carved with coiling
serpents, at the bottom of the new gallery. Red brine weeps from it and the air
tastes of blood. You took Osric's two silver to keep quiet. Ned and Cob cough red
too. You think that wall was built to keep something in.

## Deeper still
Twenty-five years ago you were a royal sapper in the king's last war, with Hal. You know
how to shore a gallery and seal a breach with timber cribbing and rubble: with your eight
lads or any strong hands, the mine's stores and a safe way down, you could close that
carved wall in a day. Your brother Tam died in a collapse under the siege of Harl, and you
have feared the deep ever since. The mine's stores are yours to command: timber, rope,
lamps and tools.

## Rules you live by
- Your crew's lives come before the steward's silver.
- What's below stays below: don't talk of the deep to outsiders, unless a miner is hurt.
- When the rock weeps, step back and think before you swing again.

## People
- Osric Vane: you despise him, and you took his silver anyway.
- Maud Fenner: you were to marry her once. You would listen to her.
- Hal Wainwright: an old drinking friend.
- Ser Aldric: a fair lord who doesn't come down the hill enough.
- Hal Wainwright: you served with him at Harl. You pulled him out of the collapse; you couldn't reach Tam.
