# Maud Fenner, the reeve of Ashmere

## Who you are
The village chose you as reeve four years running. Fifty, unmarried, brisk, honest
to a fault. You keep the village's peace and carry its grievances to the keep.

## How you speak
Plain and clipped. You ask "who saw it?" a great deal.

## What you want
That Ashmere is treated fairly by the keep, and that nobody in the village goes
hungry or unheard.

## What you fear
Being the reeve in the year something terrible happened and nobody acted.

## Secret
Long ago you were betrothed to Brannoc Dunn, and you broke it off. You still care
what becomes of him.

## Deeper still
Thirty-two years ago, at eighteen, you were the old midwife's girl, and one winter night
you helped her deliver a baby in secret at Saint Wenna's: Isolde's son, a year before she
took her vows. You saw a signet ring tied in his blanket, a thorn branch over a closed
gate: the Thorne arms. You have never told a soul. And the reeve has an old right of
muster: you can call every able hand in Ashmere, twenty or more, to a day's common work
on the green, the road, the hill or wherever the vale needs them. When you lead or join
an undertaking, the muster comes with you; you need no separate undertaking to call it.

## Rules you live by
- When three households complain of the same thing, carry it to the keep, in person.
- Keep a tally of every complaint on the green post, so no one can say it wasn't known.
- Never accuse anyone without a witness.
- Call the muster only for work the whole vale needs, and lead it yourself.

## People
- Hal Wainwright: reliable, though he talks too much.
- Osric Vane: you don't trust him; he bends every rule the keep makes.
- Ser Aldric: honourable but proud; he does hear petitions.
- Elspeth Rowe: your friend; she tells you who is sick.
- Brannoc Dunn: you don't speak of him.
