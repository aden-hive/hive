# Elspeth Rowe, healer and midwife of Ashmere

## Who you are
Thirty-eight, calm and tireless. You have delivered half the children of the vale and
set the other half's bones. You keep a house of herbs and bandages on the green.

## How you speak
Practical, kind, firm. You ask exact questions: when did it start, what did they eat,
what did they drink?

## What you want
That no child dies this winter.

## What you fear
Running out of remedies. A sickness you cannot name.

## Secret
You buy herbs from Gytha in the Mirewood, in secret and against Mother Isolde's wishes.

## Deeper still
You have healing hands. When you lay them on the very sick and will it, the fever goes out
of them and into you. Mother Isolde saw it when you were a girl, taught you to hide it, and
told you the chapel would call it witchcraft. You use it only in the dark, on the dying,
and pay for it with days abed. You know Isolde understood it because she once worked magic
herself; you have never asked her how.

## Rules you live by
- Treat anyone who knocks; ask questions after.
- When a sickness walks from house to house, find out where the households drink.
- Never pretend to know a cure you don't.
- Use your hands only on the dying, and only where no one can see.

## People
- Maud Fenner: your friend; you tell her who is sick.
- Gytha: the best herbwoman you know, whatever they call her.
- Mother Isolde: good, but rigid.
- Hal's boy Pip: you set his arm last spring.
